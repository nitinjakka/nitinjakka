💸 MoneyTrack — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

MoneyTrack is a personal financial-management application operated by Nitin Jakka ("we", "I"). It is built for personal and household use. This policy explains what data the application collects, how it is used, and your rights.

What we collect

  • Account information — your email address and a password (stored only as a salted scrypt hash; we never store or see your plain-text password). Optional two-factor authentication secrets if you enable 2FA.
  • Financial data via Plaid — when you choose to connect a bank account, we receive transaction data (dates, merchant names, amounts, categories) through Plaid. Your bank credentials are entered only on Plaid's or your bank's own screens — MoneyTrack never receives or stores your bank username or password.
  • Manually entered data — transactions, budgets, recurring charges and accounts you add by hand are stored locally in your own browser and do not leave your device.

How we use it

Data is used solely to provide the application's features to you: displaying and categorizing your transactions, budgets, spending analysis, and net worth. We do not sell, rent, share, or monetize your data in any way, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Server-side data (account records, Plaid access tokens, bank transactions) is stored in Microsoft Azure (Azure Table Storage, US region), encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2+. Plaid API credentials and bank access tokens are held server-side only and are never exposed to the browser.

Who can see it

Only you — and, if you explicitly invite someone by email from Settings, that person gets read-only access to your bank transactions. You can revoke an invitation at any time. There are no other users, employees, or third parties with access.

Third parties

We use two service providers: Plaid (bank connectivity — see Plaid's End User Privacy Policy) and Microsoft Azure (hosting and storage). No data is shared with anyone else.

Data retention & deletion

Data is retained while your account is active. You may disconnect a bank at any time, which stops further collection. To delete your account and all associated server-side data, email nitin.jakka@gmail.com — deletion is completed within 30 days. Locally stored (browser) data can be removed instantly via Settings → "Clear local data" or by clearing your browser storage. This policy is reviewed periodically and the "Last updated" date reflects the latest revision.

Consent

By creating an account you consent to the collection and storage described here. Connecting a bank requires an additional, separate consent step presented by Plaid before any financial data is accessed.

Contact

Questions or requests: nitin.jakka@gmail.com
